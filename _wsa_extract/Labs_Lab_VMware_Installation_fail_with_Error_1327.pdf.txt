VMware Installation fail with Error 1327. Invalid Drive Error: 
Reason: 
The System Registry may be erroneously pointing to a temp drive. 
Solution:  
1. Back up your Registry. 
2. Select Start> Run> and then type Regedit. 
3. Navigate to the following folder:  computer 
heky_local_machine\software\wow6432node\vmware\vmwareplayer\installpath64 
4. In the right panel, search for entries that list the incorrect drive.  
5. Modify the incorrect drive to point to C:\.
